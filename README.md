# Conversion IQ

**Stop guessing why your pages aren't converting. Get an AI audit in minutes.**

Conversion IQ is a WordPress plugin that analyses your pages, scores them across six conversion factors, and tells you exactly what to fix — with rewritten copy ready to use.

---

## What it does

You install it, point it at your pages, and it comes back with:

- **An overall conversion score** (0–100) so you know where each page stands
- **Six category scores** — Clarity, CTA Strength, Emotional Impact, Readability, Engagement, and Trust — so you know *why* the score is what it is
- **Prioritised recommendations** — quick wins you can action today, plus longer-term improvements
- **AI-rewritten copy** for your headlines, CTAs, and key sections — ready to copy and paste
- **A shareable report** hosted at [conversioniq-app.com](https://conversioniq-app.com) — send it to your client or team in one link

## Key features

**Audit & Score**
- Scores every published page on your site in one click
- Weighted scoring model — not all factors are equal
- Detects content that hasn't changed since the last audit

**Reports**
- Shareable public report URL — no login required for clients
- Score history tracked over time so you can show progress
- Overview dashboard with trend charts and page rankings

**Automation**
- Schedule audits to run daily, weekly, or monthly
- Audit history persists in the cloud — survives plugin reinstalls

**Lead Intelligence (KnockKnock)**
- See which companies are visiting your pages
- Visitor identification with company name, industry, and contact details
- Month-over-month visitor and lead trend tracking

**White-label**
- Custom company name, logo, and accent colours
- Reports branded to your agency, not ours

## Getting started

1. Download and install the plugin via **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**
2. Activate and go to **Conversion IQ** in your WordPress admin menu
3. Enter your licence key on the **Settings** tab
4. Fill in your business context (industry, target audience, goals) for more accurate audits
5. Head to the **Audits** tab and run your first audit

**Don't have a licence key?** Get one at [conversioniq-app.com](https://conversioniq-app.com)

---

## Works with everything

Conversion IQ reads your page content regardless of how it was built — Gutenberg, Elementor, Divi, or any other page builder. If it's a published WordPress page, it can be audited.
