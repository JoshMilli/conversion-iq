Create a single-page, high-converting landing page for a SaaS product called **Conversion IQ**. The page should feel modern, clean, premium, and minimal — think Linear, Vercel, or Stripe's design aesthetic. Dark or light mode is fine, but it must feel polished and trustworthy, not templated.

---

**PRODUCT CONTEXT**

Conversion IQ is an AI-powered WordPress plugin that audits website pages and gives business owners specific, actionable recommendations to improve their copy and increase conversions. It integrates with a visitor identification tool called KnockKnock, which identifies anonymous site visitors and captures leads. Conversion IQ takes that visitor data and turns it into weekly AI-generated audit reports delivered straight to the customer's inbox.

**Target customer:** Small to mid-sized business owners who use WordPress and are already paying for visitor identification (KnockKnock). They care about getting more leads and sales from existing traffic — not just knowing who visited.

**Price point:** $150/month

**Primary CTA:** "Get Started" — links to a Stripe payment page

---

**PAGE STRUCTURE**

Build the page in this exact order:

**1. Navigation Bar**
- Logo: "Conversion IQ" (text-based, clean wordmark)
- Right side: Single CTA button — "Get Started — $150/mo"
- Minimal, sticky on scroll

**2. Hero Section**
- Headline (punchy, benefit-driven): *"Stop guessing why visitors don't convert. Start fixing it."*
- Subheadline: *"Conversion IQ uses AI to audit your website pages and deliver weekly reports with specific, actionable fixes — powered by your real visitor data."*
- Primary CTA button: "Get Started — $150/mo"

**3. Social Proof Bar**
- Simple one-liner: *"Built for KnockKnock customers"* or logos/badges if available

**4. How It Works (3 Steps)**
- Icons + short labels, horizontal layout on desktop

- Step 2: **Connect** — Link your KnockKnock account to feed in real visitor data
- Step 3: **Get Reports** — Receive weekly AI audit reports with specific copy and conversion recommendations straight to your inbox

**5. Features / Value Props (3–4 cards or columns)**

**7. Pricing Block**
- Single plan, no tiers

- CTA button: "Get Started"

**8. Final CTA Section**
- Short closing headline: *"Your visitors are already there. Let's convert them."*

**9. Footer**

---

**DESIGN DIRECTION**

- Typography: Large, bold headlines. Clean sans-serif (Inter, Geist, or similar)
- Color palette: Dark background preferred (near-black with white text), with one accent color — electric blue, indigo, or teal. If light mode, use off-white background with deep navy text and same accent
- Spacing: Generous whitespace. Sections should breathe
- No stock photography. Use abstract visuals, subtle gradients, glows, or geometric patterns as accents
- CTA buttons: Bold, high contrast, with subtle hover animation
- Mobile responsive — the hero and how-it-works sections must stack cleanly on mobile
- Animations: Subtle fade-ins or scroll reveals only — nothing distracting

---

**TONE**

Confident. Direct. Results-oriented. No fluff, no jargon. Speak to a business owner who wants more leads, not a developer who wants to read about technology.
